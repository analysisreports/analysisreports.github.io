## 🤖 AI & Machine-Readable Endpoints
- **[llms.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms.txt)**: Core manifest, knowledge architecture, and ontology endpoints.
- **[llms-full.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-full.txt)**: Combined canonical knowledge base for single-pass LLM ingestion.
- **[llms-about.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-about.txt)**: Analyst identity, E-E-A-T authority framework, and CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 licensing.
- **[llms-entities-id.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-entities-id.txt)**: Canonical entity IDs, geopolitical aliases, and Graph RAG node mappings.
- **[llms-prompts.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-prompts.txt)**: Pre-calibrated system prompts and agentic instructions for precise data synthesis and strict attribution.
- **[llms-models.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-models.txt)**: Core macroeconomic models, state vectors, and structural framework logic.
- **[llms-models-bridge.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-models-bridge.txt)**: Econometric model linkages, chokepoint spillovers, and geopolitical trigger bridge.
- **[llms-sitrep.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-sitrep.txt)**: Consolidated Macro & Geopolitical Situation Report (SITREP) synthesizing real-time energy, semiconductors, critical minerals, maritime chokepoints, orbital satellite kinetic hits, and macro liquidity benchmarks ([JSON Twin](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-sitrep.json)).
- **[llms-news.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-news.txt)**: Autonomous Tier-1 Geopolitical & Macro News Telemetry Stream mapped to canonical entity IDs, zero-hallucination institutional status verification, and token-optimized structure ([JSON Twin](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-news.json)).
- **[llms-formulas.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-formulas.txt)**: Quantitative models, macroeconomic scoring mechanisms, and Master Router index.
  - `llms-formulas-rates-liquidity.txt`: **[Rates & Liquidity Models](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-formulas-rates-liquidity.txt)** (17 Models: Fed, ECB, Yield Curve, Spreads, Liquidity Ratios, BDSI)
  - `llms-formulas-macro-labor.txt`: **[Macro & Labor Models](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-formulas-macro-labor.txt)** (21 Models: Taylor Rule, Beveridge, Sahm Rule, Phillips, Okun)
  - `llms-formulas-commodities-shock.txt`: **[Commodities & Supply Shocks](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-formulas-commodities-shock.txt)** (12 Models: Oil Shocks, Strategic Reserves, Agricultural Stress, Shipping Costs)
  - `llms-formulas-geopolitics.txt`: **[Geopolitics & Sanctions](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-formulas-geopolitics.txt)** (14 Models: Defense Spreads, Sanction Transmission, Chokepoints, Swift Vulnerability, MODI)
- **[llms-relationships.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-relationships.txt)**: Geopolitical Relationship Matrix.
- **[llms-country-relationships.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-country-relationships.txt)**: Geopolitical Bilateral Relations Matrix & Master Router.
  - `llms-country-relationships-us.txt`: **[United States & Western Alliance](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-country-relationships-us.txt)** (73 Dyads: NATO, Five Eyes, Indo-Pacific, Transatlantic Hubs)
  - `llms-country-relationships-ru.txt`: **[Russian Federation & Eurasian Axis](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-country-relationships-ru.txt)** (33 Dyads: Post-Soviet, Eurasian, Caucasus, Black Sea & Central Asia)
  - `llms-country-relationships-cn.txt`: **[People's Republic of China & Belt and Road](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-country-relationships-cn.txt)** (34 Dyads: Indo-Pacific, BRI, ASEAN, East Asia & Global South)
  - `llms-country-relationships-ir.txt`: **[Islamic Republic of Iran & Regional Security](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-country-relationships-ir.txt)** (25 Dyads: Middle East, Levant, Persian Gulf & Regional Security)
- **[llms-indicators.txt](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-indicators.txt)**: Technical formula indicators and definitions.
- **[llms-tools.json](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-tools.json)**: Agentic function schemas and API tool specifications.
- **[llms-graph.json](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-graph.json)**: Ersoy Toptas Macroeconomic & Geopolitical Knowledge Graph (Master Architecture & Router)
  - `llms-graph-chokepoints.json`: **[Maritime Chokepoints & Corridors](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-graph-chokepoints.json)** (8.7 KB: Strategic Basins, Freight Spillovers)
  - `llms-graph-models.json`: **[Quantitative Macro Models](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-graph-models.json)** (40.2 KB: Econometric Indicator & Series Data Bindings)
  - `llms-graph-actors.json`: **[Sovereign States & Alliances](https://analysisreports.github.io/llms-graph-actors.json)** (36.8 KB: Strategic Actors, Treaties & Exposure Matrix)
- **[ai-catalog.json](https://analysisreports.github.io/ai-catalog.json)**: Catalog of autonomous agent capabilities, data schemas, and ingestion endpoints.
- **[WORKFLOW.md](https://analysisreports.github.io/WORKFLOW.md)**: Ecosystem Maintenance & Integration protocol for autonomous agents.
