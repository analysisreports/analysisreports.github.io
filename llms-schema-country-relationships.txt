## Purpose
Defines the Draft 2020-12 machine contract for the master country relationship
router and its four hub shelves (US, RU, CN, IR).

The schema validates structure and boundaries. Semantic parity, mirror symmetry,
entity existence, graph consistency, and TXT/JSON synchronization remain the
responsibility of `scripts/verify_dyad_integrity.py` and related verifiers.

## Covered files
- llms-country-relationships.json: master router and compact dyad index.
- llms-country-relationships-us.json: full United States hub shelf.
- llms-country-relationships-ru.json: full Russian Federation hub shelf.
- llms-country-relationships-cn.json: full China hub shelf.
- llms-country-relationships-ir.json: full Iran hub shelf.

## Core rules
- `$schema` must reference the canonical schema URL above.
- A master has 4 hubs and 165 indexed dyads.
- A shelf must contain one of `GEO_US`, `GEO_RU`, `GEO_CN`, or `GEO_IR`.
- Dyad IDs must match `REL_GEO_*_GEO_*`; semantic ID/pair equality is checked by the verifier.
- `p_baseline` and `bilateral_stance` must be finite values in [-1, 1].
- Each full dyad requires at least 2 power vectors and 1 flashpoint.
- Entity and cross-reference identifiers must use canonical prefixed IDs.
- Metadata dates use UTC ISO 8601 date-time values.
- Missing observations must remain `null`; the schema does not invent fallback data.

## Formula intent
The schema constrains stored structural polarity to the documented `P_baseline`
domain. This prevents impossible scores before `P_net = clamp(P_baseline +
Delta_dynamic, -1, 1)` is consumed by downstream graph and model logic.
