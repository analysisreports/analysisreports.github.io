## 1. FRED / ALFRED GLOBAL TRANSFORMATION CODES
- lin : Levels (No transformation) | x(t)
- chg : Change | x(t) - x(t-1)
- ch1 : Change from Year Ago | x(t) - x(t - n_obs_per_yr)
- pch : Percent Change (MoM / QoQ) | ((x(t) / x(t-1)) - 1) * 100
- pc1 : Percent Change from Year Ago (YoY) | ((x(t) / x(t - n_obs_per_yr)) - 1) * 100
- pca : Compounded Annual Rate of Change (SAAR) | (((x(t) / x(t-1)) ** n_obs_per_yr) - 1) * 100
- cch : Continuously Compounded Rate of Change | (ln(x(t)) - ln(x(t-1))) * 100
- cca : Continuously Compounded Annual Rate of Change | ((ln(x(t)) - ln(x(t-1))) * 100) * n_obs_per_yr
- log : Natural Log | ln(x(t))

Observation Frequencies (n_obs_per_yr):
- Daily (d) : 260 observations/year (Business days, excludes weekends)
- Weekly (w / wef / wew / wesa) : 52 observations/year
- Biweekly (bw) : 26 observations/year
- Monthly (m) : 12 observations/year
- Quarterly (q) : 4 observations/year
- Annual (a) : 1 observation/year

Mathematical Guardrails:
- Log Guardrail: Enforce assert x(t) > 0 before applying log, cch, cca. Reject or flag non-positive values (negative real yields, deficits).

Aggregation Alignment Rule:
- Rates / Spreads / Prices (DFF, DGS10, VIXCLS): aggregation_method="avg"
- Stocks / Levels (WALCL, WTREGEN, RRPONTSYD, M2SL): aggregation_method="last" (Period-end close)
- Flows / Net Changes (PAYEMS, BOPGSTB): aggregation_method="sum"
