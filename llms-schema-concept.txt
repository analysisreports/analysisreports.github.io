[SYSTEM CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE]
This schema defines the standard ontological payload for financial concepts, technical 
indicators, economic doctrines, and political science frameworks under the "concept" category. 
LLM agents must use this contract to parse definitions, operational steps, key metrics, 
and synthetic QA pairs.
