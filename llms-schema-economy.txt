[SYSTEM CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE]
This schema defines the standard ontological payload for macroeconomic, monetary policy,
and central bank analysis posts under the "economy" category. LLM agents must use this
contract to parse indicators, forecast distributions, and synthetic QA pairs.

2. Data Types: Numerical values (rates, yields) must be cast to Float, dates to ISO 8601.
