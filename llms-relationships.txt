## 1. DYNAMIC POLARITY & TRANSMISSION ENGINE
All bilateral actor relations operate on a dual-layer state model:
Net Polarity: P_net = math.clamp(P_baseline + dynamic_delta, -1.0, 1.0)

Polarity Spectrum:
- +1.00 : Formal Mutual Defense Treaty / Integrated Command (e.g., Article 5)
- +0.50 to +0.80 : Strategic Alignment / Institutional Bilateral Partnership
- +0.10 to +0.40 : Tactical Cooperation / Economic Interdependence
-  0.00 : Strict Non-Alignment / Strategic Neutrality
- -0.10 to -0.40 : Strategic Friction / Managed Decoupling / Diplomatic Tension
- -0.50 to -0.80 : Structural Adversary / Proxy Conflict / Sanctions Regime
- -1.00 : Active Kinetic Warfare / Total Diplomatic Breakdown

Regime States (Discrete Mapping):
- ALLIANCE            : Structural alignment, intelligence sharing, collective defense.
- DETENTE             : Formal treaties de-escalating structural conflict.
- DIPLOMATIC_CONTACT  : Tactical communication without shifting structural baseline.
- PROXY_WAR           : Non-state or asymmetric conflict through third-party theaters.
- KINETIC_WAR         : Direct, state-on-state military engagements.
