[SYSTEM CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE]
This schema defines the master ontological payload for hybrid macro-geopolitical intelligence, combining
sovereign defense postures, maritime chokepoints, bilateral tension vectors, and deterministic quantitative
econometric models under the "strategic-macro" category. LLM agents must use this contract to parse 5W1H frameworks,
live central bank time series, evaluated macro models, actor friction polarities, logistics capacities, and synthetic QA pairs.
