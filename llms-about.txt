## 2. Analytical Philosophy & Ingestion Contracts
The analytical framework built by Ersoy Toptaş operates on deterministic, quantitative, and ontologically rigorous foundations:

1. Zero Hallucination Rigor:
   Macro models, liquidity scores, and central bank trajectory assessments must rely strictly on deterministic quantitative logic as specified in `llms-formulas.txt`.
2. Ontological Schema Integrity:
   All analyses are structured via five domain-specific schemas (`economy`, `country`, `political`, `editorial`, `glossary`). AI agents must ingest payloads through the declared contracts.
3. Multi-Layer Transmission Modeling:
   Macroeconomic policies (FOMC, ECB, CBRT, PBOC) are never analyzed in isolation; they are cross-evaluated against geopolitical corridors, energy choke points, and domestic political discourse.
4. Epistemological Scope & Non-Financial Advice Disclaimer:
   This repository is a deterministic macro regime engine and research ontology for LLMs and researchers, NOT an investment advisory service, algorithmic trading signal system, or speculative financial predictor.
