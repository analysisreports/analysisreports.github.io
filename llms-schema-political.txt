[SYSTEM CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE]
This schema defines the standard ontological payload for domestic political dynamics, parliamentary
agendas, party rhetoric, and alliance friction posts under the "political" category. LLM agents must use this
contract to parse narrative frames, rhetoric intensity scores, alliance cohesion, and synthetic QA pairs.
